# 2026-10-21

## Course names from the agent's title

**What changed**: a new course was named by the learner's raw typed goal, so typing "springboot?" titled the course "springboot?". The roadmap now takes an optional `title` (core §5: at most 60 characters; old records without one stay valid, `format_version` unchanged), and the research and roadmap briefs and the `save_roadmap` tool ask for the course's name in a few plain words, with no language named. The desktop names a course `title`, else its goal; research no longer stores the typed words, so only a course from before titles keeps them (`names`); the learner's edits and the saved sources keep the title. The goal screen's "isn't set up yet" notice waits for typing to pause 600 ms, so it no longer names half-typed words. Core DESIGN §5 and desktop DESIGN §3.2 and §4.13 say so.

Before the title exists, the research heading, its job row and the goal notice show the typed goal tidied by `renderer/ui/topic.ts` ("springboot?" reads "Springboot"), and a course badge takes its name's first grapheme, capitalized when that stays one letter. The helper lives in `shared/topic.ts`, so the mock titles a course with it too. A course with a stored typed name keeps it even once a change adds a title, and the notice matches the tidied goal against track names.

**Verified**: core typecheck, build and unit tests; desktop typecheck and the main, learn, app and course tests on macOS. New tests: the schema's title, `getRoadmap` returning it, the briefs asking for it, the name order with the title kept through approve and source saves, an untitled course named by its goal, a blank title refused, and the notice not showing for "Data s" mid-typing.

**Not verified**: a real agent writing a good title (the springboot stretch of the video is re-recorded after this merges).
