# 2026-10-31

## Activities for any topic: proposal and phase 1

A Harness Engineering roadmap showed that Codemm practises only code, loses its research after the roadmap, and adapts pacing but not content. The proposal is [docs/proposals/activities-for-any-topic.md](../proposals/activities-for-any-topic.md): Code, Quiz or Lessons-only courses, research that records practice and pitfalls, a quality bar, and the learner's misses reaching the author. An Opus agent reviewed it, and its findings are applied. The owner's open questions are in its §8.

Phase 1 (§3.1, §3.6, and §3.4's lesson line), on `feat/research-quality`. Astra wrote the core and desktop parts; Claude wrote the briefs and reviewed Astra's diff.

- **Core.** Roadmap concepts take optional `practice`, `pitfalls` and `refs`. The roadmap gate checks that refs are in range, and that with sources, a concept with practice or pitfalls has a ref. `next_step`'s author step carries `practice` and `pitfalls`.
- **Briefs.** Research uses primary sources and recent ones in fast-moving fields, records the three fields per concept, and folds concepts no activity can practise into a neighbour's lesson. Lessons follow one teaching structure: objective, why, small steps, worked example, mistakes, self-check, sources. Authors practise the `practice` line and write wrong solutions from `pitfalls`. A track may hold a kit for topics about tools or systems. A new `review` brief checks a roadmap against its sources.
- **Desktop.** One review job runs after research, in a fresh session with web access. Research stays running until it ends, and a failed review still shows the roadmap. Roadmap edits merge by concept id, so the new fields survive Approve and edits.
- **Docs.** DESIGN §5, §6 and §8 (including `sources` in `get_roadmap`, which §8 had left out), and desktop DESIGN §3.2, §4.9 and §4.11.

### PR #269 review fixes

The coordinator's review found five problems. Astra fixed them, and Claude reviewed the diff and added one more guard.

- **The review could overwrite the learner's roadmap.** While a course's review runs, its draft isn't offered, and any roadmap save (approve or edit) is refused with "still being checked".
- **A hung review could cost 20 more minutes.** The review now gets one attempt with a 5-minute deadline, through a per-job `relaunch: false`.
- **An empty `pitfalls: []` needed refs.** An empty list now counts as absent.
- **Stale lines** said only research and setup may search the web. They now include review, and keep the reason only research shows web steps.

## Phase 2: Lessons only

Implemented on `feat/lessons-only`, without committing, pushing or switching branches. Courses accept `style: none` with an optional retained track. Separate pure reading progression handles coverage, prerequisites and skips; exercise operations refuse, while history, mastery, lessons and notes survive switches. MCP and CLI expose reading progress. Desktop offers Code or Lessons only, schedules lessons without setup or authors, shows lessons read, and has an Activities switch on the course map. The switch restores the saved coding style or requests a track through the roadmap job. Shared setup failures cannot stall a lessons-only course. DESIGN docs updated. Existing examples keep their recorded style; new examples while activities are off use the retained track's first offered style.

## Phase 3: What the learner missed reaches the author

A `check` event now records `missed`: the visible tests that failed, by name, and the hidden ones, by index, at most 50 each, taken from the same redacted view `check` returns. A perfect check leaves it out, and older events still parse. `next_step`'s author step carries it in `last`, and the author brief tells the author to aim the next activity at the mistake.
